Welcome to Universal High School Kisaasi

We are a day and boarding secondary school in Kisaasi, Kampala, offering O-level and A-level education. Our approach is learner-centred: we embrace every student's uniqueness and help them discover who they are.

Our vision is to guide each learner to become a leader who transforms society. We are committed to providing the best education in an environment that supports that journey.
