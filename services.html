How to apply

  1. Get an admission form

    Pick one up from the school office in Kisaasi, or call or WhatsApp us and we will tell you how to get one.

  2. Return the form with the requirements

    Bring the completed form together with the documents listed under admission requirements.

  3. Pay fees and report

    Pay the first term's fees and report on the reporting day, between 8:30 am and 6:00 pm.
