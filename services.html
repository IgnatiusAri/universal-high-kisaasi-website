Programmes and admissions

O-level and A-level, as a day or boarding student.

Admissions are open. See how to apply

Our programmes

O-level

Senior One to Senior Four, following the competence-based curriculum, with daily lessons and preps.

Tuition: UGX 600,000 day, UGX 900,000 boarding

A-level

Senior Five and Senior Six. Applicants choose their subject combination on the admission form.

Tuition: UGX 640,000 day, UGX 940,000 boarding

Day school

Day scholars attend lessons and return home in the evening. Lunch is provided at school.

Tuition from UGX 600,000

Boarding

Boarders live at school, with dormitories, a sick bay with a school nurse, and supervised evening preps.

Tuition from UGX 900,000

How to apply

  1. Contact the head teacher's office

    All admissions are handled by the head teacher. Call or WhatsApp the school, or visit us in Kisaasi, to get the admission form.

  2. Fill in the personal record form

    Attach photocopies of the results slip (PLE for O-level, UCE for A-level), a recommendation or the last report card, and four passport photos. A-level applicants state their subject combination.

  3. Pay fees at the bank

    All fees are paid into the school's bank account. At least 50% of the fees must be paid before a student is admitted.

  4. Report on the first day of term

    Report between 8:30 am and 6:00 pm, register at the receiving desk and collect a clearance card signed by the bursar. Bring the requirements list for checking.

Paying fees

  • All fees are paid into the bank. The school is not responsible for money paid to any member of staff.
  • No student is admitted without paying at least 50% of the school fees.
  • Pocket money is deposited with the bursar. A student may keep no more than UGX 5,000.

Requirements

Bring these on the reporting day. The list is checked on arrival.

Office requirements (all students)
  • Former report card, and the UCE or PLE results slip
  • A ream of ruled paper, 500 sheets (S4, S5 and S6)
  • A ream of printing paper, Rota Trim or Dolphin only (S1, S2 and S3). No other type is accepted.
  • 1 kg of Omo
  • 6 rolls of toilet tissue (big size)
  • Four passport photos: 1 of the parent, 2 of the student and 1 of the guardian
Personal requirements
  • Counter books: 12 for A-level, 15 for O-level
  • A mathematical set, at least 5 pens and 2 pencils
  • A basin, a jerrycan and a school bag
  • A drinking water bottle
  • Toothpaste (3 medium tubes)
  • Four packets of sanitary pads (girls)
Boarding students
  • 4 rolls of toilet tissue and a packet of razors
  • Mattress, blankets, two pairs of bed sheets, a towel and a mosquito net
  • Night dress and half-slip (girls), night gown (boys)
Casual and weekend wear
  • Girls: a plain black long Caribbean skirt, a long black half-slip and a red T-shirt
  • Boys: plain black trousers and a blue T-shirt
  • A plain black coat, sweater or jacket (no hood)
  • 4 pairs of long stockings: white for girls, black for boys
  • Black open shoes or crafts only
  • The casual set is available from the school at UGX 60,000, and sports wear at UGX 47,000

Good to know before joining

  • Students must report within two weeks of the reporting date, or the place is given to someone else.
  • Students arriving after 6:00 pm on reporting day are not received.
  • Mobile phones, radios, cameras, chargers and similar gadgets are not allowed and are confiscated.
  • School uniform with black low-heeled shoes is worn in class, on outings, and when leaving or returning to school.
  • Meals are compulsory. Day scholars are given lunch.
  • Services are held on Thursdays and Sundays for Christian students and on Fridays for Muslim students.
